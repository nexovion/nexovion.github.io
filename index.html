01 — ABOUT

Two brothers. One AI universe.

NEXOVION started as a small experiment between siblings and grew into a full creative-tech brand.

NEXOVION began as a late-night idea between two brothers who wanted to build something that didn't exist yet — a brand where AI, storytelling, and design merge into one universe. No big studio, no funding. Just two people, a laptop, and a vision of the future.

What started as a single animated episode is now becoming a growing world of characters, artwork, and systems — each piece built by hand, shipped in public, and improved in the open.

Every update to this site is part of that same experiment: build small, ship fast, upgrade constantly.
